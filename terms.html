Terms of use (draft)

This starter draft is not legal advice. The project operator must review it and add current support details before wider use.

Team use

Push Pottaa sends short Therivili messages to a team Slack channel in response to configured GitHub events. Tell channel members before enabling it and provide a way to mute or remove the integration.

Acceptable use

Use it for consensual team humour about code, bugs, builds, and deployments. Do not use it to harass, threaten, discriminate against, or target a person’s identity or personal vulnerabilities. Do not include credentials, private source code, or sensitive personal data in event details.

Availability and contact

The service is provided as an early MVP and may change or become unavailable. Add the operator’s support contact and review these terms for the intended jurisdiction before public launch.
