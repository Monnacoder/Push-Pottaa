Terms of use (launch draft)

This is a starter draft, not legal advice. Replace it with terms appropriate to your business and jurisdiction before taking payments or launching publicly.

Entertainment only

Commit Crimes is intended for consensual team humour. Workspace administrators should tell team members that roast messages are enabled and allow users to mute or opt out.

Acceptable use

Do not use the service to harass, threaten, discriminate against, or target a person's protected traits or personal vulnerabilities. Roasts should focus on code, bugs, build events, and fictional situations. Do not send secrets, credentials, private source code, or sensitive personal data in webhook payloads.

Availability

The early prototype is provided as-is and may change or be unavailable. Paid plans, billing, refunds, cancellation and service commitments must be documented before payment features are enabled.
